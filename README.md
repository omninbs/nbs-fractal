It is a concrete implementation of the [formal reduction of redstone music](DESIGN.md), pioneeringly validating the feasibility of its formal logic and layout structures.

> This crate is in early development with a frequently changing API. Please pin your dependency to a specific version.

## Scope of this work

The main part of this crate is the **analysis and reconstruction of repetitive structures**. As a research branch of formal reduction, the formal rule of repetition has a relatively intuitive representation. But as one kind of formal rule, it does not represent the whole of formal reduction.
